## Product Engineer verification

- Search filters the queue by incident content and updates the visible-result count.
- A non-matching query renders a distinct no-results state and can be cleared without resetting the demo.
- Selecting an incident opens a detail drawer without navigating away from the queue.
- A Lead can assign an owner and the new owner persists in the queue row.
- An Analyst sees static owner information, a disabled save action, and an explicit permission explanation.
- Closed incidents show static owner information with a closed/read-only label for every role.
- Loading shows table-shaped skeleton rows.
- Empty queue is distinct from a failed request.
- Backend error exposes Retry; successful retry restores the queue.
- Mobile viewport renders the compact filter entry point and incident-card presentation.

## Review outcome

The wireframe is approved as behavior-complete input for Design Inspiration and UI Design. Visual styling remains provisional and may change without altering the product contract or validated behavior.
