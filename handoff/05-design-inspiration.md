## Visual directions

1. **Operational Light** — white and cool-gray surfaces, blue actions, compact table-first layout, restrained severity accents.
2. **Midnight Command** — near-black surfaces, monospaced identifiers, luminous status colors, dense command-center character.
3. **Calm Control** — slate-tinted light surfaces, generous grouping, accessible contrast, and selective emphasis on critical events.

## Designer decision

Select **Calm Control**. It preserves the wireframe's fast scanning and high-density queue while avoiding the visual fatigue of a fully dark command center. Borrow only abstract principles: compact hierarchy, restrained status color, clear grouping, and operational typography. Product behavior, role permissions, state transitions, and information architecture remain unchanged.
