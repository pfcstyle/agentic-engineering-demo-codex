# Product Contract Approval

## Review outcome

The Incident Triage Dashboard product contract and Figma Make planning handoff are approved for wireframe production.

## Coverage review

- The primary operational goal is observable: locate an urgent incident, inspect it, and assign an owner when authorized.
- Lead and Analyst capabilities are separated explicitly.
- Closed incidents remain read-only for every role.
- Filters, search, ordering, results count, drawer context, and ownership persistence have testable outcomes.
- Loading, empty queue, no filter results, backend error, retry, save progress, and save success are independently demonstrable.
- Desktop, tablet, and mobile transformations preserve the critical queue information and actions.
- Keyboard access, focus handling, accessible names, live announcements, contrast, and non-color cues are part of the contract.
- Out-of-scope editing, production integrations, administration, analytics, and collaboration are explicitly excluded.

## Wireframe entry criteria

Figma Make may enter Build only after its Plan response maps every required behavior and state to a deterministic prototype control or mock-data condition. Any missing permission, recovery, responsive, or accessibility behavior must be resolved in Plan mode before Build.
